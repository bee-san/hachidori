## Goal

The headword becomes a rail of kanji tiles (stroke ring, the reading this word uses, JLPT/grade/strokes, top meanings); the kanji view is an atlas card with "Met on this page". Port it from the onRender prototype to its own popup on the v1 contract (c26/c28): build from the model, never scrape or hide Default.
